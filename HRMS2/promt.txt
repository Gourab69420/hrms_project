4. PAYROLL REQUIREMENT — IMPORTANT
==================================================

There is one explicit modification to the payroll requirement:

BONUS must be included under ALLOWANCES.

Do NOT create a separate Bonus entity unless I explicitly request it later.

Payroll should conceptually calculate:

Basic Salary
+ Allowances
+ Bonus
- Deductions
= Total Salary

Treat Bonus as a component of the employee's payroll allowances.

The existing Payroll entity already contains:
- basic_salary
- allowances
- deductions
- total_salary
- status

Keep the ER structure intact while supporting Bonus as part of the allowance calculation.
